# ENTITY consolidated 14-division architecture register

**Authority:** This is the complete, permanent division register for the unified ENTITY ecosystem. All 14 divisions are part of one system. The eight-division list formerly shown in the product blueprint was incomplete; it is not a replacement for this register.

| # | Division | Scope |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | **ENTITY MIND** | Intelligence, reasoning and knowledge. |
| 2 | **ENTITY REALITY** | AR, VR, MR, 3D environments and human interaction. |
| 3 | **ENTITY HUMAN** | Biology, medicine, genetics, neuroscience and human regeneration. |
| 4 | **ENTITY SCIENCE** | Physics, mathematics, chemistry and scientific research. |
| 5 | **ENTITY ENGINEERING** | Engineering, robotics, materials and technological design. |
| 6 | **ENTITY SPACE** | Astronomy, spacecraft, orbital mechanics and space exploration. |
| 7 | **ENTITY PLANET** | Earth systems, climate, environment and ecosystems. |
| 8 | **ENTITY FUTURE** | Predictive modelling, emerging technologies and invention. |
| 9 | **ENTITY NANO** | Nanotechnology, nanoscale materials and molecular engineering. |
| 10 | **ENTITY QUANTUM** | Quantum science, quantum computing and quantum simulations. |
| 11 | **ENTITY REVERSE** | Reverse engineering of software, hardware and physical systems. |
| 12 | **ENTITY CODE** | Programming, software engineering, AI development and debugging. |
| 13 | **ENTITY EVOLVE** | Controlled continuous learning, evaluation and Owner-approved self-improvement. |
| 14 | **ENTITY CORE** | Central orchestration, identity, authorization, security and governance. |

The division identifiers used by the authorization service are the corresponding lowercase slugs: `mind`, `reality`, `human`, `science`, `engineering`, `space`, `planet`, `future`, `nano`, `quantum`, `reverse`, `code`, `evolve`, and `core`. The policy rejects unknown identifiers. No division may be removed, renamed, merged, or silently repurposed; changes to its scope require explicit governance review and an audited policy change.
